Add document no longer opens the camera. It opens "What are you adding?" first; after a kind is chosen, a short "what to show" screen (with a lighting tip) has one primary **Start camera**. The chosen kind sets what the camera reads (license: barcode, passport: MRZ, card: card scan). The camera and its preview are released when you leave the screen, go back or switch apps, and Android Back still steps back one level at a time.
