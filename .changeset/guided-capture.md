Scanning a card, licence or passport is now a guided scan, like a bank's. A fixed outline in the right shape for what you chose (licence or ID card, payment card, passport page) sits in the middle of the camera, and plain words say what to change: Move closer, Move back a little, Fill the outline, Hold steady, Too much glare, Too dark, Hold the phone flat, Show the back. When the document is lined up, steady and sharp for about half a second the photo is taken by itself with a buzz (the round button is always there), as a full-resolution still where the phone offers one. The photo is cut to the outline and set upright; Rotate stays on the review. The outline no longer chases the document, so it no longer jumps. Photo and the phone's own document scanner work as before.
