HumID 3.0 (provisional): new module for whole-computer backups. Sealed pieces under one backup key; the key opens with any two of three parts (HumID vault part behind a live check plus keyshare, printed backup sheet, trusted contact's part). Keyed piece ids, sensitive-file hold-back, scan hook, part replacement without re-encryption. See docs/BACKUP.md.
