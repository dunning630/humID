**3.0 gets the guided scan and the faster capture from 2.2.0.** A card, licence, ID card or passport page is lined up inside a fixed outline in the right proportions; plain words say what to change (Move closer, Move back a little, Fill the outline, Hold steady, Too much glare, Too dark, Hold the phone flat, Show the back); when it is lined up, steady and sharp for about half a second the photo is taken by itself as a full-resolution still where the phone offers one, cut to the outline and set upright (Rotate stays on the review). The Add > ID camera carries a **Card / Passport page** switch, because it does not know yet which it will be shown. The camera still asks for the long side only, so no browser crops its view. Capture to review is faster: the page is straightened from the part of the frame it is in, the clean scan is made in a worker after the review opens (Seal waits for it only if the clean scan is the chosen version), card text is read from a smaller image, the passport read is seeded from the live read, the location is read beside the encryption (up to three seconds, was five), and the sealing arithmetic warms up while the review is on screen.
